most asked questions

what is your delivery timeline?
around 7-10 business days, give or take, unless some wacky stuff happens.
what are your payment modes?
we currently accept bank transfers, jazzcash, easypaisa, and cash on delivery for most cities.
what are your shipping charges?
standard shipping is Rs. 250 nationwide. we offer free shipping on all orders over Rs. 5,000!
do you ship worldwide?
currently, we only light up homes within pakistan, but we hope to expand soon.
what's the best way to care for your products?
always trim your wick to 1/4" before lighting. for sculptural candles, place them on a dish to catch the wax.
do you make custom candles?
yes! we love creating custom colors for events and weddings. slide into our DMs to discuss.
